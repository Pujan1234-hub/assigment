02 / Diagnostics

FixCheck

In development
Open web app ↗

When a website or service stops working, most people do not know whether the problem is the device, Wi‑Fi, DNS, the internet connection or the service itself. FixCheck is meant to narrow that confusion quickly.

01No forced account wall for the core check.
02Uses browser-available network signals instead of pretending to see everything.
03Explains likely causes in plain language.
04Ends with a practical next step instead of raw technical output.

03 / Public safety

FloodSafe Nepal

Active build
Open latest web app ↗

FloodSafe Nepal is focused on one country and one practical question: what is happening around the place that matters to me right now? The product direction is local relevance, clearer station freshness and a map made for Nepal instead of a generic world view.

01Nepal-only map direction with nearby river/station context.
02Official river observations and warning information where available.
03Visible update timing so stale data does not look live.
04Local-area alerting instead of unrelated national noise.
