# DateMate Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 4 September 2026**

DateMate is a grocery expiry reminder app. This policy explains what information DateMate uses and how it is handled.

## Information stored on your device

DateMate can store the following information locally on your device so the app can work:

- Your first name or chosen display name.
- Grocery/product names.
- Expiry dates.
- Product barcodes.
- Reminder preferences and app settings.
- Item status such as active, used, or archived.

This information is stored locally on your device. DateMate does not require an account and does not operate its own cloud database for this information.

## Camera and expiry-date scanning

DateMate uses the device camera to scan product barcodes and printed expiry dates. Expiry-date images are processed on the device for text recognition. DateMate does not intentionally upload or store camera photos on a DateMate server.

## Product lookup

When you scan a barcode, DateMate may send the barcode number to the public Open Food Facts service to look up a product name. This request requires an internet connection. Open Food Facts may receive technical information normally associated with an internet request, such as an IP address, according to its own policies and infrastructure.

DateMate does not sell barcode information or use it for advertising.

## Notifications

DateMate uses local device notifications to remind you when groceries are close to expiry. Reminder information is generated from data stored on your device.

## Information DateMate does not intentionally collect

DateMate does not intentionally collect:

- Precise or approximate location.
- Contacts.
- SMS or call history.
- Financial or payment information.
- Health information.
- Advertising identifiers for targeted advertising.
- Account passwords.

DateMate does not contain third-party advertising and does not use an analytics service in the current version.

## Data sharing

DateMate does not sell personal information. The only routine external data transfer in the current version is the barcode lookup request described above. Third-party services process information under their own privacy terms.

## Data retention and deletion

Grocery information and preferences remain on your device until you delete items, clear the app's data, or uninstall DateMate. Because DateMate does not require an online account, there is no DateMate cloud account that needs to be deleted.

## Children's privacy

DateMate is a general utility app and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

## Security

DateMate is designed to keep grocery and reminder information on the device wherever practical. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when DateMate's features or data practices change. The latest version will be made available at this page.

## Contact

For privacy or support questions, please use the developer support contact shown on DateMate's Google Play listing.
